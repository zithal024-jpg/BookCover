# BookCover privacy

BookCover does not collect, send or share any information.

- **No internet.** The app does not request Android's internet permission, so it cannot
  connect to any server. Online images or files that a book tries to load are blocked.
- **No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.**
- **Your books stay on your phone.** Imported EPUB and PDF files are copied into the app's
  private storage, which other apps cannot read. Reading positions, bookmarks, collections,
  reading history, relics and settings are stored there too.
- **No cloud backup.** Android's automatic cloud backup and device transfer are switched off
  for BookCover. A backup only exists if you choose **Save backup** and pick where to save
  the file. Backup files can contain book titles, reading dates and bookmark names, so keep
  them private.
- **Diagnostics stay local.** BookCover keeps a small technical log on the phone, designed to
  leave out book text, file locations and image contents. It is never sent anywhere; it can only be viewed on the
  phone through a hidden diagnostics screen.
- **Uninstalling** BookCover deletes everything it stored.

If you send feedback, you decide what to include. Please don't include your own books or
screenshots of their text.
